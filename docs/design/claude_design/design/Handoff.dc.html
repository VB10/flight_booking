4 · Decisions

Cart BadgeIconButton lives in AppTopBar, not a floating button. It stays visible while the list scrolls, cannot overlap a card’s Add to Cart row or the Snackbar, and matches where the existing app already put it. Theme and language moved to Profile › Settings. They are rarely changed, and the Flights top bar keeps three actions (cart, profile, log out) that fit at 390 pt with Turkish titles. Remove from cart uses a trailing icon plus swipe-to-dismiss. The icon is the accessible path; swipe is the shortcut. Undo in the Snackbar covers accidental removal. New semantic roles. successContainer, warningContainer, infoContainer, their on-roles, onSuccess, onWarning and scrim were added so AppSnackbar, StatusBadge, InfoBanner and the warning AppDialog never use one-off colors. Contrast pairs are at least 4.5:1 in both themes. Added components. AppChip, AppSearchField, AppAvatar, AirlineMark, RouteVisual, InfoBanner, SummaryBar, SettingsRow, AppSwitch, SegmentedToggle, LoadingOverlay. All appear on pages 0b and 7. Text growth. Labels sit in flexible rows with min-width 0 and about 30% spare width; buttons in FlightCard use flex 1 so “Sepete Ekle” fits as easily as “Add to Cart”.
