Roles with an on-prefix are the foreground for the role they pair with. Container roles (primaryContainer, secondaryContainer, successContainer …) are the tonal fills used by StatusBadge, AppSnackbar, EmptyState and InfoBanner. The scrim behind dialogs is black at 50% (light) / 60% (dark).

2 · Spacing scale

{{sp.n}}
pagePadding = 20 (horizontal)

Scale: 8 · 10 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32. 8 = icon-to-label and button-to-button gaps, 10 = inner card rows, 16 = card padding and gap between cards, 20 = page padding, 24 = dialog padding and section gaps, 32 = state illustrations and hero spacing.

App headings map onto the scale: h1 → headlineMedium, h2 → headlineSmall, h3 → titleLarge, h4 → titleMedium. PriceTag uses titleLarge with fontWeight 700 (small: titleMedium · large: headlineLarge size), the one sanctioned weight override.
